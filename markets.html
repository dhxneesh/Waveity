TECHNOLOGY / MARKETSA PATH FROM RESEARCH TO REAL-WORLD USE

First, the evidence.
Then, the open sea.

Our initial market focus is offshore oil and gas. Aquaculture, offshore data centres and other marine users are potential later applications, guided by technical validation and site needs.

WHY START OFFSHORE?

Bring the energy source
closer to the need.

Offshore operations need dependable electricity. Waveity is exploring whether local wave energy can contribute alongside existing power systems, with the aim of reducing dependence on transported fuel.

Our development pathway

01RESEARCH UNDERWAY

Refine & test

Develop the float, mechanics and hydraulic system. Compare simulations with laboratory behaviour.

02PLANNED

Validate offshore

Work towards pilot partnerships to evaluate reliability and performance in real sea conditions.

03LONGER-TERM AMBITION

Replicate & expand

Use validated results to guide modular manufacturing and deployment across offshore applications.

WHAT VALIDATION MUST ANSWER

Performance. Reliability.
Practicality at sea.

How much usable electricity can an integrated system deliver? How will it withstand marine conditions and be maintained? What will installation, operation and integration cost at a specific site?

These questions shape the next stage of work. Commercial output, offshore reliability and project economics have not yet been demonstrated.
