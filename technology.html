Promising in simulation.
Building the evidence.

Our research uses ANSYS AQWA modelling alongside scaled prototypes to explore how the system responds to different waves.

The results describe simulated energy capture, not end-to-end electrical conversion or measured offshore output. Integrated-system validation and sea trials remain ahead.
