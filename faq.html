What is Waveity developing?

A floating wave-energy system designed to capture ocean motion and convert it into electricity for offshore users. The research combines moving floats, adaptive mechanics and hydraulic energy transfer.

Explore the system ↗
Is the technology already deployed commercially?

The work is at the research and prototype stage. Laboratory prototypes and simulations inform development; offshore pilot validation and commercial deployment are future steps.

Explore the development pathway ↗
What does the 52.6% result mean?

It is a simulated energy-capture efficiency result from the team’s ANSYS modelling, reported for modelled waves of 2.5–3.9 m. It is not a field-measured efficiency, a capacity factor, or a guarantee of electricity output at a customer site.

Review all simulation conditions ↗
Can it replace all of an offshore site's existing power?

That has not yet been demonstrated. Site conditions, demand, storage, backup supply and system integration would need to be assessed before defining the role of wave power at a particular location.

Who can collaborate with Waveity?

We welcome enquiries from offshore operators, pilot-site partners, marine engineers, research collaborators and investors interested in supporting the transition from laboratory research to offshore validation.

Start a conversation ↗
What still needs to be demonstrated?

Integrated electrical output, offshore reliability, maintenance requirements, site-specific economics and lifecycle environmental performance all need further validation. Laboratory results guide that work; they do not yet establish commercial performance.
