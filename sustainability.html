A different current.

Our ambition: help offshore operations draw energy

FLOAT AT WATERLINE / CONCEPT VISUALISATION

Decarbonisation
starts with the energy.

Waveity is exploring how wave energy could reduce dependence on transported fossil fuels for offshore electricity. The opportunity motivates our research; the impact needs to be measured.

01 / ENERGY

Use the resource nearby.

Explore wave motion as a renewable input close to potential offshore users.

02 / INTEGRATION

Work as part of a system.

Assess demand, storage, backup supply and connection requirements to understand where wave energy can contribute.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Consider the full picture.

Materials, manufacturing, installation, maintenance and end-of-life all belong in the assessment of future environmental performance.

WHAT WE NEED TO PROVE

Ambition, backed by evidence.

Reliable energy capture. Practical offshore operation. A measured environmental benefit. These are questions for further development and validation.

Waveity has not established a measured lifecycle carbon footprint or verified emissions savings for a commercial installation. We do not claim a quantified carbon reduction at this stage.
